What is an ingredient? An ingredient is a canonical food item in the knowledge base — for example chicken_breast or brown_rice. Each ingredient has a unique slug ID (lowercase letters and underscores), a food group (meat, vegetable, cereal…) and a state indicating whether the reference values are for the raw, cooked, or processed form. Ingredients are the building blocks used by the ML service: raw nutritional profiles are looked up by ingredient ID and then transformed by cooking relationships.
