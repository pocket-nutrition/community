What is a cooking relationship? A cooking relationship links a raw ingredient to a cooking method (boiled, grilled, steamed…) and records two key values:

• Yield factor: the mass ratio of the cooked product to 100 g raw. A value of 0.750 means 100 g raw → 75 g cooked (water lost during cooking). A value above 1.000 means the ingredient absorbs liquid (e.g. rice, pasta).
• Confidence (0–1): how reliable the value is. Values close to 1.0 come from official food composition tables (Ciqual, USDA); lower values are community estimates.

The ML service uses these relationships to convert raw nutritional profiles into post-cooking predictions.
